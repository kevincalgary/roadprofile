Every self-reported relationship to a vehicle is labeled "Not verified by RoadProfile" wherever it appears. Information may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. ## 3. No ownership claims, no exclusive control

Creating a vehicle profile does not establish or prove ownership of that vehicle, and does not give the creator exclusive control over the profile. Any authenticated user in good standing may contribute to any valid VIN. Users may edit or delete their own contributions; they may not silently overwrite another contributor's record, though anyone may suggest a correction or dispute through the moderated correction process.

## 4. Not a marketplace

RoadProfile does not host vehicle sales, listings, bidding, or payments. "Sale or auction" history records document that an event happened (e.g., "sold at a dealer auction on [date]"); they are not listings and cannot be used to buy, sell, or bid on a vehicle.
