# RoadProfile Vehicle History Disclaimer

This exact text is shown on every vehicle history summary (`app/vehicle/[vin]/summary.tsx`) and exported PDF:

> RoadProfile contains crowdsourced information that may be incomplete or inaccurate. It is not a substitute for a professional inspection or an official vehicle-history report.

## Where this appears

- Vehicle history summary page, above the fold
- Exported PDF summary, below the vehicle title
- [Recommend also surfacing a shorter version in the app footer / vehicle profile page, and in any marketing referencing "vehicle history" — confirm placement with counsel]

## What it needs to communicate (for counsel's review)

1. RoadProfile's data is user-submitted and not independently verified by RoadProfile.
2. It is not equivalent to, and should not be relied on in place of, a paid vehicle-history report (e.g., from a title/history data provider) or a physical pre-purchase inspection.
3. RoadProfile does not guarantee completeness — a lack of recorded damage, accidents, or issues is not evidence the vehicle has none.

Counsel should confirm whether additional disclaimer language is needed regarding: liability for purchase decisions made based on RoadProfile data, the "community submitted" vs. "verified" data-status distinction shown on every vehicle profile, and any jurisdiction-specific consumer-protection requirements for platforms that host vehicle-condition information.
