# RoadProfile Community Guidelines (Draft)

_Last updated: [DATE] — replace before publishing_

RoadProfile exists to build an honest, useful public record of vehicle history. These guidelines explain what that requires of everyone who contributes.

## Contribute honestly

- Only post what you know or can reasonably support. Don't fabricate maintenance history, mileage, damage, or specifications.
- Label your relationship to the vehicle accurately (current owner, former owner, mechanic, dealer, family member, enthusiast, or witness). Every self-reported relationship is shown to readers as "Not verified by RoadProfile" — that's expected, not a flaw, but it makes honesty your responsibility.
- If you're not sure a detail is right, say so in the description rather than presenting a guess as fact.

## Protect people's privacy

Never post:

- A person's full name in a context that identifies them personally, home address, phone number, or personal email address
- Government identification numbers
- Payment or financial account information
- Signatures visible on uploaded documents
- Information that could help someone locate or contact a vehicle's owner

Before publishing a receipt, invoice, or document, RoadProfile prompts you to redact this information and strips photo/document metadata (like GPS location) automatically — but you're responsible for what's visible in the image itself. If you see someone else's post containing personal information, use "Report sensitive information" — moderators prioritize these reports.

## Don't use RoadProfile to find or contact someone

RoadProfile is not a people-search tool. Do not use vehicle history, VIN records, or messaging to locate, contact, or pressure a vehicle's current or former owner outside of RoadProfile's own reporting and correction tools.

## No unsupported accusations

You can document a dispute (e.g., a mileage inconsistency is automatically flagged, not accused) but don't post claims that someone committed fraud, theft, or a crime without clear, verifiable evidence. Use the correction/dispute process instead of accusatory language in a public record.

## This isn't a marketplace

Don't use RoadProfile to advertise a vehicle for sale, solicit offers, or conduct a transaction. Documenting that a sale or auction happened is fine; running one here is not.

## Respect other contributors

- No harassment, threats, or targeted abuse.
- No impersonation of another person, dealer, or business.
- No spam — repetitive, irrelevant, or promotional content unrelated to a vehicle's actual history.

## Corrections, not edit wars

Anyone can suggest a correction to a vehicle's identity fields (VIN, year, make, model, trim, specifications) with a reason and supporting evidence. These go to moderator review before taking effect — you can't silently overwrite another contributor's record, and neither can anyone else.

## What happens if you don't follow these guidelines

Depending on severity, RoadProfile moderators may:

1. **Warn** — a private note explaining what needs to change.
2. **Remove content** — the specific record, comment, or reply is taken down; a note remains in the moderation history.
3. **Suspend** — temporary loss of the ability to contribute or message.
4. **Ban** — permanent loss of account access.

Every moderation action is logged (`moderation_actions`, `audit_logs`) with the reason given. If you believe a decision was made in error, contact [support contact — finalize with product/legal] to appeal.

## Reporting

Use the report control on any post, comment, reply, vehicle, list, user, or message. Report categories map directly to what moderators see first: spam, harassment, sensitive personal information, incorrect VIN, duplicate vehicle, inappropriate content, impersonation, and unsupported accusations.
